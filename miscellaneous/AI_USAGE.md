# AI Usage

## Week 4 — M2 Story Slice

AI was used to help understand the Week 4 worksheet and lecture concepts, including personas, scenarios, user stories, acceptance criteria, and Given/When/Then criteria and provide md format of the worksheet.

AI was also used to review the draft story slice for completeness and identify sections that still needed to be completed.

The team made the final decisions about the product direction, stories, acceptance criteria, priorities, edge cases, and scope.


## Week 5 - Architecture v1
Claude was used to help draft the Architecture v1 document.

AI was used to review the alignment between the M2 requirements and the proposed structure, including the S1 user story, S1-AC1, S1-AC2, the API error case, component responsibilities, and design decision D1.

The team made the final decisions about the architecture structure, component responsibilities, design alternatives, chosen option, quality attribute, trade-off, assumptions, scope, and review changes. The team also performed the final peer review and verification.
